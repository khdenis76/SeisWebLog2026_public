ROI Editor update
=================

Added:
- Zoom In and Zoom Out buttons.
- 100% (actual image pixels) button.
- Fit Image button.
- Live zoom percentage display.
- Mouse-wheel zoom centred under the pointer.
- Double-click to fit the complete image.
- Horizontal splitter between the image panel and ROI panel.
- Remembered splitter position between OCR Studio sessions.

ROI coordinates continue to use original image pixels and are not changed by
display zoom.
